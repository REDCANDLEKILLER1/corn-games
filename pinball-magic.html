How to Play

Desktop: Z / ← left flipper  •  / / → right flipper  •  hold Space to launch  •  X nudge

Phone: tap the FLIP buttons — or tap the left / right half of the table.

1. Launch — hold LAUNCH (or Space) to charge the plunger, release to fire. Full power sends the ball around the orbit and onto the board through the opening at the top left.

2. Flip — keep the ball alive with the flippers. Drain = lose the ball. You get 3 balls.

3. Spell M-A-G-I-C — roll through the 5 glowing star lanes up top. Complete the word for 5,000 pts and +1 bonus multiplier (up to 5×).

4. Mind the magic — the ball is a glowing corn kernel 🌽. The cyan and pink portals are linked: dive into either one and you always warp safely to the top center of the table, above the bumpers — never to a spot where you can drain. The wizard hat 🎩 in the middle swallows the kernel and warps it to that same safe spot up top (500 pts).

5. Cast the CORN spell — light the C-O-R-N targets around the hat. All four lit = GRAVITY FLIPS for a few seconds: the kernel falls up. Ride it to the top lanes, then gravity flips back.

6. Bonus Machine 🪙 — every time your score crosses another 100,000 points (100k, 200k, 300k…), the screen blinks and flashes, then a second pinball machine opens for 20 seconds (watch the countdown up top). Smash the ball into the gold coins to collect them — each coin is worth 500 × your bonus. The bonus table has no drain — its bottom is sealed shut, so the ball can never leave and you keep it in play for the full 20 seconds. Grab every coin and a fresh set drops in. When the clock runs out, you're back on the main table with your score, ball, and multiplier right where you left them — and the next machine arms at the next 100k.

7. Cheat code 🤫 — tap the C in the CAST C-O-R-N row 5 times (or press the C key 5 times) to get +99,000 points on the spot. That lands you just 1,000 points short of the bonus machine — score those last points and it opens.

PRO TIPS

  • Aim for the orbit on launch — passing all 5 star lanes lights letters fast.
  • Dead-flip: let the ball bounce off a still flipper to slow it down and gain control.
  • Nudge (sparingly!) when the ball heads for an outlane.
  • The wizard hat is a safe 500 × your multiplier, dead center — it always warps the kernel to the top of the table, never toward the drain.
  • Portal shortcut: enter low, exit high — both portals always drop you safely above the bumpers, a free ride back into play.
  • Save your last C-O-R-N letter for when the kernel is low: flip gravity and float it back up instead of draining.
  • Bonus machine coins are the fastest points in the game — a full 20 seconds of coins can carry you most of the way to the next 100k machine.
